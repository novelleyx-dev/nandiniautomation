About Us

Our Company

We are the group of Intellectuals and Professional operating as a Team under one roof, by the name ‘Nandini Enterprises’, the company which was established in the year 2006 and is forging ahead with innovative measures catering every need of Electrical, Instrumentation & Automation Sectors. As an ISO 9001:2008 and CPRI Certified Company, our organization has been the most RELIABLE source for general purpose instruments and sophisticated automation systems for many Corporate Giants such as Yaskawa, Honeywell, Baumer and Yokogawa who are progressing the Infra and thus the economy of India.

Business follows Excellence!

Bringing Excellence in Business relationships and values, our company is a perfect blend of ‘highly professional Sales Engineers’ and ‘Strong Technical Service Engineers’, continuously motivated to produce Excellence that overtake the estimations and expectations of our esteemed clientele.

Right from Designing, Manufacturing, Erection and commissioning and Installation, we take pride in disclosing our capabilities to render customised services in every related sector such as Hardware, Software & Firmware.

We have been on the path of transforming our Company as one of the most Powerful and Trusted source for all Instrumentation, Automation and Electrical Solutions.

In the Voyage of transforming we were Awarded several times for our achievements:

  • Outstanding Achievement award for Business Excellence” from International Achievers Conference in 2018

  • Fastest Growing Indian Company Excellence Award” from Indian Economic Development & Research Association in 2017

  • Best Innovation Award from L&T in 2010

  • Best Women Business Partner Award to Ms. Pratima Maruti Marlapalle from L&T in 2011

  • Management Development Program in IIM Lucknow in 2011

We extend our warm Welcome to you to experience the Edge of Changing Future!
